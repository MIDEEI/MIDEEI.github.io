My Favorite Pizza

Backstory

Pizza started in Naples, Italy. People used flatbread with tomatoes, cheese, and herbs. Over time, pizza became popular everywhere. I like pizza because it tastes great and comes in many flavors.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F.
  2. Spread the sauce on the dough.
  3. Add cheese and pepperoni.
  4. Bake for 12–15 minutes.
  5. Eat and enjoy!
